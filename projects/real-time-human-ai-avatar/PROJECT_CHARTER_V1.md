# Project 1 — Real-Time Human AI Avatar Platform

Status: ADMISSION / RESEARCH ONLY
Owner: Investments Espana LLC
Director mandate: investigate, challenge assumptions, design, cost, legally admit, plan and benchmark before any significant new spend.

## Objective
Design and validate an ultra-realistic conversational AI avatar platform for continuous real-time audiovisual interaction approaching the sensation of speaking with a real person.

## Candidate routes
A. Tencent 3D Digital Human — premium realism reference / quality ceiling.
B. Qwen + MiniMax Speech + Alibaba LiveAvatar — priority first validation; balance realism, voice, continuity, latency and integration.
C. Qwen + MiniMax Speech + Taobao3D/on-device — scalability, full-body, phone capture, edge/mobile and marginal-cost reduction.

The Director MUST consider hybrids and alternatives. No route is pre-approved.

## Decision objective
Maximize: human sensation × stability × scalability × economic margin.

Decision must be one of: GO / NO-GO / GO WITH ARCHITECTURE CHANGE / GO HYBRID.

## Human-sensation acceptance
Target >=95/100 jointly across voice, prosody, conversational latency, lips, mouth/teeth, eyes, blink, gaze, facial expression, head, gestures, body where applicable, continuity, identity stability, artifact absence, and language/emotion/motion coherence.

95/100 without economically sustainable cost is NOT approval.

## Required pre-investment deliverables
1. Updated technical research: Tencent Digital Human, Qwen, MiniMax Speech, LiveAvatar, Taobao3D and relevant alternatives.
2. Capability/limitation matrix.
3. E2E architecture: user -> capture -> STT/VAD -> LLM -> memory -> TTS -> avatar -> streaming.
4. Component and total latency budget.
5. Persistent identity/memory architecture.
6. Continuous conversation and natural turn-taking design.
7. Emotion, gaze, gesture and non-verbal behavior design.
8. Personalized avatar and eventual full-body strategy.
9. GPU infrastructure per route.
10. Reproducible GPU benchmark.
11. Legal Admission: OSS/model licenses, voice, biometrics, likeness/image rights, privacy, commercial use.
12. Technical/economic/legal/vendor risks.
13. Dependency-aware phased roadmap.
14. Executable backlog with acceptance criteria.
15. Specialized capabilities/agents per phase.
16. Human-sensation QA/Judge.
17. Full economic model.

## Mandatory economic matrix
For A/B/C: monthly fixed cost, GPU, LLM, STT, TTS, streaming/network, storage, cost/minute, cost/hour, low/medium/high/extreme user cost, supported concurrency, expected GPU utilization, expected realism /100, expected latency, possible customer price, gross margin, profit %, break-even users, vendor-dependency risk.

Usage profiles: low occasional; medium moderate daily; high prolonged daily; extreme multi-hour/persistent agent.

## Spend gate
No significant new GPU capacity, commercial service, closed model or infrastructure purchase before architecture -> dependencies -> benchmark plan -> cost -> margin -> risks -> success criteria are presented and owner-approved.

Before any new GPU rental, provide provider, GPU, VRAM, hourly price, maximum authorized runtime, exact hypothesis, required evidence, and automatic shutdown/hibernation procedure.

## Legal / safety admission
Treat voice, face, biometric templates, likeness and persistent identity as high-sensitivity data. Require explicit consent/provenance for cloning or personalized identity; prohibit impersonation without authorization; minimize retention; separate identity secrets from conversational memory; document deletion/export paths; assess applicable biometric/privacy/consumer-protection rules before public launch.

## First Director order
Analyze this project, investigate and validate all three routes, design the E2E architecture, execute Legal Admission, generate roadmap/backlog/dependencies, select capabilities/agents, and present execution and cost plan before new spending. Produce the decisive low/medium/high/extreme cost table, expected realism, latency, break-even and profit for A/B/C, then issue GO / NO-GO / GO HYBRID with evidence.

## Restriction
Do not begin full construction or contract additional GPU capacity during admission/research.
